Tube Status — Support

Crowdsourced London Underground status.

Tube Status shows what travellers are reporting across the network right now, with official TfL line status alongside. Report in two taps, confirm what others are seeing, and swipe to say something's cleared.

Common questions

My report says "Sending…" — what now? Reports file instantly and send when you have signal. Underground with no signal, they queue and send automatically when you surface.

Why don't I see every report? By default the feed shows what's relevant to you — your lines, stations near you, and anything the crowd or TfL has corroborated — so it doesn't flood. Switch to "All" to see everything on your lines.

A report looks wrong. Tap the flag control on the report to tell us, or email us. Reports also expire on their own after about 15 minutes of no activity.

How do I remove ads? Tube Status Premium removes ads — as a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime unlock. Already bought it? Use Restore Purchases on the Premium screen.
