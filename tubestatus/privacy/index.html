Tube Status — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

Tube Status is a crowdsourced London Underground status app. You file short reports about what you're seeing on the network; everyone sees the combined picture. We keep the data we handle to the minimum needed to make that work, and there are no accounts and no sign-in. This policy explains what the app handles.

What stays on your device

Your preferences — the lines you follow, pinned stations, appearance, notification settings — and the on-device relevance profile that learns which stations matter to you are stored on your device. They are never uploaded to us.

Reports you file (shared)

When you file a report, the app sends a small, fixed set of fields to our server so it can appear in the shared feed: the line, the type of issue (for example "severe crowding" or "station closed"), the station, and the direction. There is no free-text field, no photos, and nothing that identifies you. These reports are visible, anonymously, to other people using the app. Reports are short-lived: they automatically expire about 30 minutes after filing and are deleted from our systems within a few hours.

Anonymous device identifier

To stop one device flooding the feed and to keep voting to one-per-device, the app creates a random identifier the first time it runs and keeps it in the device Keychain. It is not your name, email, Apple ID, or advertising identifier, and it is not linked to your identity — it is only a random value used for rate-limiting and de-duplication.

Location (optional, off by default)

If you turn it on, the app uses your location only on your device to work out the nearest station so reports near you rank higher. Your location is not stored and is never sent to us or anyone else. You can turn it off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Official TfL status

Alongside traveller reports, the app shows official line status sourced from Transport for London (TfL) Open Data. Tube Status is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by TfL. Official status is fetched by our server, not your device, so no request is made from your phone to TfL.

Advertising

Tube Status shows ads through Google AdMob. To do this, AdMob may process a device advertising identifier and related data to serve and measure ads. On first launch the app asks for your consent — through Google's consent message where required and Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt — and you see the same number of ads either way. You can review Google's practices in the Google Privacy Policy, and disable personalised ads in your device's privacy settings. Tube Status Premium removes ads entirely.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (Tube Status Premium, available as a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime unlock) are handled by Apple. We never see or store your payment details.

Children

Tube Status is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can use the app without location and without ad tracking. Because reports carry no identifier tied to you, and everything else stays on your device, there is no account to delete; clearing the app removes the on-device data, and filed reports expire on their own.
