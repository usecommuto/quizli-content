Daily Cryptoquote — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Daily Cryptoquote is a word puzzle: each day a public-domain quote is enciphered and you decode it. There are no accounts and no sign-in. We keep the data the app handles to the minimum needed to make the game work. This policy explains what that is.

What stays on your device

Your progress — solved puzzles, streak, stats, difficulty preference, sound and haptics settings — is stored on your device, and optionally in your own private iCloud (Apple's key-value storage) so it can sync across your devices and survive a reinstall. This data lives in your device and your personal iCloud account; it is never uploaded to us and we cannot see it.

The daily quote

To fetch the day's puzzle, the app makes a plain web request to our server for that date's quote file. The request contains no personal information and no identifiers we create. As with any web request, our hosting provider may transiently log standard technical details such as your IP address for security and abuse-prevention; we do not use these to identify you.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob (a banner during play and an occasional full-screen ad between rounds). To serve ads, AdMob may process a device advertising identifier and similar data. Depending on your region you may see Google's consent form and, on iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; whether or not you allow tracking, you see the same number of ads — only how personalised they are changes. You can review Google's practices in the Google Privacy Policy, and you can limit ad tracking any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Premium subscribers see no ads at all.

Purchases

Premium is an auto-renewable subscription handled entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see or store your payment details. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above.
