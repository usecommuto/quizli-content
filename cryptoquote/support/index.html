Daily Cryptoquote — Support

We're happy to help.

Daily Cryptoquote enciphers a public-domain quote each day; you decode it by placing letter tiles. One cipher letter always stands for the same real letter throughout the quote.

Common questions

How do I play? Tap a blank cell, then tap a letter tile to place it — or drag a tile straight onto a cell. The same enciphered letter always maps to the same solution letter. Use a hint if you're stuck (allowance depends on your chosen difficulty).

What does Premium include? Premium (£0.99/month) removes ads, unlocks all Challenge Packs, and opens the full archive of past editions. It's an auto-renewable subscription billed through your Apple ID; manage or cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

I paid but don't have Premium. Open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchases. If it still doesn't appear, email us.

Will my streak sync across devices? Yes, if you're signed in to the same iCloud account — progress syncs through your private iCloud.
