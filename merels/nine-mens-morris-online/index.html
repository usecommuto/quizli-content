Play it on your phone

Rather than a throwaway browser game, Merels is a proper, hand-crafted Nine Men's Morris app built for the way people actually play now — a couple of quiet minutes a day. Every board is designed and solver-checked, the interface is calm and uncluttered, and your streak follows you.

New to the game?

Nine Men's Morris takes two minutes to learn and years to master. Start with our full guide: How to Play Nine Men's Morris — the board, the rules, how to form a mill, and the strategy that wins games.

Is there a free online version?

Merels is an iPhone app rather than a browser game — that's what lets the boards, animations and daily streak feel properly polished. The daily puzzle, the first ten library puzzles, and the game against the computer are free; an optional subscription removes ads and unlocks the full puzzle set and archive.
